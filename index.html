How to Foresee the Future with AI

A Practical Guide to Reading, Exploring, Choosing, and Shaping Tomorrow with Artificial Intelligence

by Wonju Hwangbo, Ph.D.

"The future is not one. It never arrives fully formed — it first appears as a faint signal in the present, and it is made, little by little, by the questions we ask and the choices we make today."

A hands-on guide to futures thinking for the age of artificial intelligence — beyond the old dream of predicting tomorrow. Across ten chapters and five parts, Dr. Hwangbo shows how to read change, imagine many possible futures, and choose the one we want, together with AI. This edition is fully revised for the AI era, expanding the author's earlier book into a method for thinking with AI, not just about it.

  • Read the signals of change that quietly announce the future
  • Use the core methods — horizon scanning, Delphi, scenario planning, backcasting, cross-impact analysis
  • Work with AI as a thinking partner while keeping human judgment
  • See how AI is reshaping work, learning, health, culture, and relationships
  • Watch AI, energy, and climate change one another's futures
  • Ask the governance question — who decides the future, and for whom

The Manifesto

"We publish for the reader who underlines — books built to hold up on the second pass."

Aorael launched in 2026 out of a shared complaint: most manuscripts get edited for speed, and the ones we love most need time instead. We're a new press — no long backlist, just a clear idea of the kind of books we want to spend the next decade publishing.

Every manuscript we take on gets two structural passes and a full line edit, done in the room with its author, long before it goes near a printer.

How a Book Gets Made

From inbox to first run

01

Reading

We read every agented submission and roughly a third of what arrives during our open call. Most manuscripts get a decision within twelve weeks.

02

Edit

Two structural passes and a full line edit, worked through directly with the author — not tracked changes disappearing into a shared drive.

03

Design & Print

Covers are designed in-house. First runs are offset-printed in small batches; reprints follow demand rather than a fixed schedule.

Submissions
